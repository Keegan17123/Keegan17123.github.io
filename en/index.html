About me

Bio

I began my undergraduate studies in Biomedical Engineering at Tsinghua University in 2017 and pursued doctoral studies and research in the same discipline there from 2021 to 2026. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Sichuan Academy of Medical Sciences & Sichuan Provincial People’s Hospital / School of Medicine, University of Electronic Science and Technology of China.

My research lies at the intersection of bioinformatics, epidemiology, genetics, and artificial intelligence. I investigate the genetic basis of major diseases and traits, as well as gene–environment interactions, to uncover their underlying molecular mechanisms. My work has been published in journals including Nature Communications, Nature Medicine, and npj Vaccines.
